FUN games rules

Participation is allowed only in teams of 4 players. Only one team per class is permitted to participate.

All instructions given by the organizers must be followed throughout the event.

Any form of cheating, external assistance, or unfair conduct will result in immediate disqualification.

The game may include unexpected twists or challenges. Participants should be prepared for anything.

The decision of the organizers will be final and binding.

GENERAL RULES (IT GAMES) INDIVIDUAL
  1. Participation is individual.
  2. Participants must follow all instructions given by the coordinators.
  3. No cheating or outside assistance is allowed.
  4. Mobile phones and smart devices are not permitted during the games.
  5. Participants must complete each game within the given time limit.
  6. Maintain discipline, fair play, and sportsmanship throughout the event.
  7. Any violation of the rules may lead to disqualification.
  8. The decision of the judges/coordinators will be final and binding.
TALK IN 60 3 ROUNDS
  1. One Minute Talk is a speaking challenge where participants research a given topic and speak about it confidently for one minute.
  2. The event has 3 rounds:
    Round 1 – Easy | Round 2 – Medium | Round 3 – Very Tough
  3. Research + Speaking Time:
    Round 1: 5 min + 1 min
    Round 2: 3 min + 1 min
    Round 3: 2 min + 1 min
  4. Participants must speak only on the given topic within one minute.
  5. Participants will be evaluated on confidence, clarity, creativity, communication, relevance, and time management.
  6. Qualified participants will move to the next round.
  7. The judges’ decision will be final and binding.
DANCE TALENT SHOW INDIVIDUAL
  1. Participation is allowed only for individual participants.
  2. Round 1 will consist of a 3-minute prepared choreography. Participants must learn and perform the given choreography.
  3. Based on the Round 1 performance, selected participants will qualify for Round 2.
  4. Round 2 will have a different performance format, which will be explained to the shortlisted participants at the venue.
  5. Participants must follow all instructions given by the organizers throughout the event.
  6. Any form of cheating, outside assistance, or unfair conduct will result in disqualification.
  7. The decision of the judges and organizers will be final and binding.
  8. The organizers reserve the right to modify the rules or competition procedure if necessary.
THE GREAT QUIZ SHOW - RULES TEAM OF 2
  1. Team-based reality quiz show with 2 contestants per team.
  2. Contestants must report before the scheduled start time.
  3. The show consists of 3 rounds, each testing different skills.
  4. All challenges follow the time limit announced by the host.
  5. Mobile phones, smartwatches, notes and external assistance are prohibited.
  6. Cheating, prompting or spectator assistance may lead to disqualification.
  7. Contestants must follow the host's instructions throughout the show.
  8. The host's decisions on questions, answers, timing and scoring are final.
  9. The team with the highest final score will be crowned the Champion.
